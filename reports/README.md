# Reports & Documentation Archive

This directory contains architectural reports and historical research notes created during the development and reverse-engineering discovery of ScrcpyDeX.

---

## Document Hierarchy & Canonical Sources

1. **Canonical Normative Documentation (Current & Active):**
   * [`docs/LEGAL_AND_LICENSING.md`](../docs/LEGAL_AND_LICENSING.md): Compliance framework, clean-room audit, and legal references.
   * [`docs/PROTOCOL.md`](../docs/PROTOCOL.md): Single Source of Truth (SSOT) protocol specification v1.0.
   * [`docs/reports/`](../docs/reports/): Official English architectural reports covering the server core, control channel, and unified UHID mouse client.

2. **English Architecture Reports (`reports/en/`):**
   * Mirrors the canonical architectural reports found in `docs/reports/`.

3. **Historical Research & Discovery Archive (`reports/pt-br/`):**
   * Contains raw engineering notes, logs, dumpsys traces, and exploration documents written during the initial reverse-engineering and reverse-interoperability discovery phase.
   * *Status:* **Historical Archive.** These documents reflect the early evolutionary process of the project and are preserved for auditability and educational transparency. In any case of conflict between historical notes and active specifications, the files in `docs/` take precedence.
